
    India has rapidly expanded its aviation network, growing from 74 airports in 2014 to over 160 operational airports, with several major new greenfield international and regional airports recently opened or under construction.Major New & Mega Airports (North & West India)Noida International Airport (Jewar, Uttar Pradesh): Opened its first phase in early 2026, this mega greenfield hub is designed to ease congestion at Delhi's Indira Gandhi International Airport, starting with an initial capacity of 12 million passengers annually.Navi Mumbai International Airport (Maharashtra): Designed to serve as a second major hub for the Mumbai region, this massive project began phased operational trials and rollouts with an initial capacity of 20 million passengers.Dholera International Airport (Gujarat): Under construction to serve Gujarat's upcoming smart industrial region, with an expected opening slated for late 2026.Major New Airports (South & East India)Bhogapuram International Airport (Andhra Pradesh): Being developed near Visakhapatnam by the GMR Group, this new international gateway is on an active construction track targeting operations in 2026.Shri Jagannath International Airport (Puri, Odisha): A greenfield project approved and planned to boost regional tourism and international connectivity to the coastal temple city, expected post-2026.Regional and Upcoming Domestic ProjectsKota Greenfield Airport (Rajasthan): An upcoming domestic airport targeted for 2026–2027 to ease heavy student and business travel demands in the region.Sonpur Airport (Bihar): An approved greenfield project meant to relieve operational pressures on Patna Airport, with projected completion around 2030.UDAN Regional Expansion: Under the regional connectivity scheme, smaller facilities like Datia and Satna in Madhya Pradesh are part of an ongoing national push targeting 100 new regional airports over the coming decade.
